Privacy Policy for Groep 7 Rekenen

Last updated: September 2026

Groep 7 Rekenen is an educational application designed to help primary school students practise mathematics.

Personal Information

Groep 7 Rekenen does not require users to create an account. The app does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, telephone numbers, addresses, or precise location information.

Children's Privacy

Groep 7 Rekenen is designed for educational use by children. We take children's privacy seriously and do not intentionally request children to provide personal information through the app.

Advertising

Groep 7 Rekenen does not contain third-party advertising.

Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information or share children's personal information for advertising purposes.

Technical Services

The app may use technical hosting and infrastructure services necessary to provide and maintain its functionality. Such services may process limited technical information required to deliver the service.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality of the app changes or when necessary to comply with applicable requirements. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer of Groep 7 Rekenen.
